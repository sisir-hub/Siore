Frequently asked questions

How do I place an order?

Choose a product and any available variant, set the quantity, add it to your bag and complete checkout with your name, phone and full delivery address.

Which payment methods are available?

Cash on Delivery is currently available. Online payment will be added after setup.

Can I customize a piece?

Contact Siore to confirm the available letters, sizes, colours or designs before ordering. Available product variants are shown on the product page.

How do I get an order update?

Keep the order number shown after checkout and contact Siore. Live courier tracking is not connected yet.

Can I return or exchange an item?

See our Return & Exchange Policy. Report damaged or wrong items within 24–48 hours with an unboxing video.

How do I care for my jewellery?

Follow the care instructions listed on your product page. If a product has no instructions yet, contact Siore before using cleaning products.
