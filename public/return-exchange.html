Return &
Exchange Policy

Every Siore piece is made with care. Please review the following policy before placing your order.

Handmade variations

As our pieces are handmade, small variations in colour, size or finishing may occur. These natural variations are part of the handmade process and are not considered defects.

Returns after delivery

We do not accept returns after successful delivery, except for damaged or incorrect items reported under the conditions below.

Damaged or incorrect items

If your order arrives damaged or you receive the wrong item, please contact Siore within 24–48 hours of receiving your parcel. An unboxing video is required so we can review the issue and arrange the appropriate return or exchange.

Share your order details and unboxing video through Siore’s official Facebook or Instagram page, or use the details on our Contact page.
